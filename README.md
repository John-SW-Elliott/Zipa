### Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 2026*

Zipa is a file archiving tool that works **fully offline**. This policy describes how data is handled.

#### 1. Data the app uses
Zipa works with the files you choose: the archives you open and the files you compress, split, join, edit or extract. To do this, it reads those files on your device. It also stores, on the device, your settings (such as language, theme, colors and default values), the list of recently opened files and, if you leave it on, a history of the operations you perform.

#### 2. Where the data stays
**On your device only.** The app has no server, creates no account, asks for no login and does not access the internet. There is no advertising, no usage analytics and no crash reporting. Zipa does not upload your files or settings anywhere.

#### 3. Passwords
The passwords you type to protect or open an archive are used only during that operation, in the device’s memory. They are never saved, never logged and never sent anywhere. For this reason, they cannot be recovered.

#### 4. Files with other people’s data
The files you handle may contain personal data of other people (Brazil’s Law 13.709/2018 — LGPD — and equivalent laws elsewhere). Whoever opens, creates or shares those files is responsible for that use. The developer **has no access to your files and is not a processor of the data in them**, because they never leave your device through Zipa.

#### 5. Saving and sharing
Files leave the app only when **you** choose: by saving them to a folder you pick or by sharing them with another app through the system’s share menu. From then on they are under your control and subject to the rules of wherever you send them.

When another app shares a file with Zipa, Zipa may keep a temporary copy in its private storage while working on it. These copies are deleted automatically.

#### 6. Permissions
Zipa requests **no permissions**. Files are reached through the system’s file picker, which gives the app access only to the files and folders you select.

The app does **not** request internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone, notifications, full storage or installed-apps access.

#### 7. Deletion
Erasing the history in the app removes it immediately. Uninstalling the app erases its settings, history and temporary copies. Files you saved in your own folders stay there until you delete them. There is no server copy — so the developer cannot recover anything either.

#### 8. Children
Zipa is a general-purpose tool and is not directed to children. It collects no data from anyone, including minors.

#### 9. Changes
Changes to this policy are published on this page, with the update date at the top.

<a id="deletion"></a>

### How to delete your data

There is no account and no server copy, so deletion happens on your device:

- **History:** erase it, or turn it off, in the app’s history screen.
- **Everything the app stores:** uninstall the app, or clear its data in your device’s app settings.
- **Your own files:** delete them with your file manager; Zipa keeps no copies of them.

O aplicativo **não** pede acesso a internet, localização, contatos, câmera, microfone, notificações, armazenamento completo nem à lista de aplicativos instalados.

Apagar o histórico no aplicativo o remove na hora. Desinstalar o aplicativo apaga as configurações, o histórico e as cópias temporárias. Os arquivos que você salvou nas suas pastas continuam lá até você apagá-los. Não há cópia em servidor — por isso, nem o desenvolvedor consegue recuperar nada.

O Zipa é uma ferramenta de uso geral e não é direcionado a crianças. Ele não coleta dados de ninguém, incluindo menores de idade.

As mudanças nesta política são publicadas nesta página, com a data de atualização no topo.

Die Dateien, mit denen Sie arbeiten, können personenbezogene Daten anderer Personen enthalten (brasilianisches Gesetz 13.709/2018 — LGPD — und entsprechende Gesetze anderer Länder). Wer diese Dateien öffnet, erstellt oder teilt, ist für diese Nutzung verantwortlich. Der Entwickler **hat keinen Zugriff auf Ihre Dateien und ist kein Auftragsverarbeiter der darin enthaltenen Daten**, da sie Ihr Gerät über Zipa niemals verlassen.

#### 5. Speichern und Teilen
Dateien verlassen die App nur, wenn **Sie** es entscheiden: indem Sie sie in einem Ordner Ihrer Wahl speichern oder über das Teilen-Menü des Systems mit einer anderen App teilen. Ab dann stehen sie unter Ihrer Kontrolle und unterliegen den Regeln des Ortes, an den Sie sie senden.
