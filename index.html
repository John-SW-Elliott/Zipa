1. WHAT ZIPA IS

Zipa is a file archiving tool that works entirely on your device. It creates, opens, checks, extracts, edits, compares and repairs ZIP files, with or without a password; splits files into parts and joins them back, including multi-volume ZIP archives; opens tar, gzip, bzip2, xz, lzma, zstd, 7z and ISO 9660 files in read-only mode and converts them to ZIP; and processes several files at once. It needs no account, no internet connection and no device permission: files are chosen through the system's own file picker.
